INFINITY GROUP DESIGN

Infinity Group is a Vietnamese design brand established in 2009. Currently we have offices in Hanoi, Saigon, Atlanta (USA). Through the creativity and craftsmanship of Vietnamese people, we want to elevate the space with special and inspiring design by collaborating with a number of experienced and qualified designers and architects.

LYEROSIS – PRODUCT DESIGN STUDIO

Lyberosis is a studio operating in the field of research and development of creative products, deeply imbued with the cultural, historical, and humanistic imprints of Vietnam.

With a criterion of placing the foundation of national culture at the forefront, we aspire to introduce a diverse range of products to the market, from board games and furniture to fragrances and essential oils that carry the quintessence of Vietnam.

1209 ARCHITECTS – LOCAL ESSENCE DESIGN FIRM

1209 Architects is a design studio operating in the field of architecture and interior design, with a core design philosophy that embraces the inheritance of traditional values, culture, and local elements, harmoniously combined with modern development and the globalization of the design industry.

INFINITY HOMES – THE MOST METICULOUS DESIGN FIRM

Infinity Homes is a design firm specializing in delivering modern, refined designs with intricate details throughout the design development process. Professionalism and experience in project and financial management are also core values of the company.
